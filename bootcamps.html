Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need prior coding experience?

No! Our courses are designed for beginners. We start from the basics and guide you to advanced concepts.

Is the training live or recorded?

It's a mix. We have live sessions twice a week for mentorship and Q&A, plus recorded lessons you can watch at your own pace.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes, we offer flexible installment plans. You can pay in up to 3 installments.

Will I get a job after the bootcamp?

While we can't guarantee a job, we provide extensive career support, including portfolio reviews, resume workshops, and introductions to our hiring partners.
